Who I am

Army officer, Operations Research and Systems Analyst (ORSA), and data scientist with 13 years of service and an active TS/SCI clearance. My interest in solving complex problems began with a strong foundation in math and science while studying Civil Engineering at Oregon State University. That background eventually led me to pursue data science, culminating in an M.S. in Applied Data Science from the University of Southern California. My operational experience spans integrated air defense, leading Soldiers, serving in Korea, deploying to Bahrain and Iraq, and supporting wargaming at the division, corps, and joint levels. Today, I bring those operational and technical experiences together to apply data science, AI, analytics, and experimentation to complex joint-force challenges.

What I do

I translate operational problems into technical solutions by designing effective workflows and connecting the right data, analytics, and practical tools to solve them. In my current role, I support joint force experimentation, wargaming, AI adoption, and strategic planning while enabling better decision-making under compressed timelines.
